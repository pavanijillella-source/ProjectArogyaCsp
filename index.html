Project Overview

Introduction & Strategic Approach

🏥

What is Health and Hygiene?

Health and hygiene are essential components of a safe, healthy, and sustainable community. While health refers to physical, mental, and social well-being, hygiene includes everyday practices and environmental conditions that prevent disease transmission and ensure clean living environments.

🎯

Project Purpose

Study health & hygiene conditions, identify unhygienic surroundings, conduct surveys with local residents, document field observations, and compile a comprehensive 7-week report.

🚀

Our 4-Stage Methodology

01

Initial Field Visit

Preliminary site visit to assess general environmental conditions and survey feasibility.

02

Assessment & VRO Approval

Identified sanitation concerns and secured official approval from the Village Revenue Officer (VRO).

03

Survey & Field Observation

Conducted household surveys (Weeks 2–5) to record waste, water, and hygiene concerns.

04

Documentation & Showcase

Compiled field data, prepared the formal CSP report, and engineered this digital showcase.

Executive Summary

Abstract

Project Arogya is a Community Service Project focused on understanding and assessing health, hygiene, sanitation, and environmental conditions within a local community. The project aims to identify common hygiene-related challenges and understand the factors that influence the health and well-being of community residents. Through field visits, community surveys, direct interactions, and systematic observations, information was collected regarding cleanliness, waste management, sanitation practices, and the overall hygiene conditions of the surrounding environment.

Project Outcomes

Conclusion

Project Arogya provided valuable practical insights into community health challenges through direct field surveying, household interactions, and official administrative coordination with the Village Revenue Officer (VRO).

By systematically mapping hygiene conditions and compiling field data, this 7-week initiative strengthened our civic responsibility while highlighting the vital role of environmental cleanliness in public health.
